# How Day by Word chooses its plans

The criteria every plan in the catalog meets are published for readers at
[daybyword.com/criteria](https://daybyword.com/criteria) (BUILD.md P16.5).
Their words are the `criteria.*` keys in `web/src/lib/strings.ts`, the one
copy, so the page and this repository never disagree; change them there.
The rules behind them are `docs/editorial.md` (review) and
`docs/catalog-format.md` (what the compiler checks).

In brief: every reference is checked; coverage is computed, never written
by hand; the workload is realistic and called an estimate; the instructions
are clear; each plan has a recorded basis for use (catalog/SOURCES.md, shown
at daybyword.com/sources); limitations stand beside advantages; and each
plan is reviewed before it is published. Day by Word does not rank plans by
popularity, recommend them with artificial intelligence, or interpret
Scripture.
