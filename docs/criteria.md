# How Day by Word chooses its plans

*This page becomes the public criteria page in BUILD.md P16.5. It is
written for readers.*

Day by Word offers a small number of reading plans and tries to tell you
plainly what each one is. A plan is in the catalog only if it meets all of
these.

**Every reference is checked.** Each reading is checked against the
chapters and verses of the Bible before the plan is published. A plan with
a reference that does not exist is not published.

**Coverage is stated exactly.** Each plan says whether it reads the whole
Bible, whole books, or passages chosen from some books. These claims are
computed from the plan's own readings, never written by hand: a plan that
says it reads the whole Bible reads every verse, and its page shows any
verses it repeats and anything it leaves out. Finishing a plan is not the
same as reading the whole Bible, and Day by Word never treats it as if it
were.

**The workload is realistic.** Each plan shows roughly how long its
readings take, from the lightest to the heaviest, counted from the number
of words and typical reading speeds. These are estimates: reading slowly,
reading aloud, stopping to reflect, or listening all change them.

**The instructions are clear.** Each plan says how to follow it, which days
it expects you to read, and what happens if you miss a day.

**We can show why we may offer it.** Each plan has a recorded basis for
use, listed on the sources page. Day by Word's own plans are labeled as
original. A plan from another source is offered only with that source's
permission or when it is in the public domain, and it is credited as the
source asks.

**Limitations are stated beside advantages.** Each plan's page says whom it
suits and what it does not do, so you can choose with the whole picture.

**Each plan has been reviewed.** Before it is published, each plan is
checked against these criteria and against its own rules, and its
description is checked for plain, neutral wording. The review level is
shown on the plan's page.

Day by Word does not rank plans by popularity, does not recommend plans
with artificial intelligence, and does not interpret Scripture or tell you
what to believe. It is independent and not affiliated with any church or
denomination. If you find a mistake in a plan, please tell us at
support@daybyword.com, with the plan, the reading, and what is wrong.
