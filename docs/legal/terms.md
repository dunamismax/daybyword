## What Day by Word is

Day by Word is a Bible reading companion: it helps you choose a reading
plan, shows what to read today, and keeps a record of what you have read.
It is free to use and needs no account. Using the website or the app means
you accept these terms.

## Your own Bible

Day by Word shows references, never the text of the Bible. You read in
your own Bible: on paper, in an app, or as audio. Links to other services
(such as YouVersion and Bible.com) are destinations, not endorsements, and
those services' own terms apply when you use them.

## Plans, not advice

Plans and their descriptions are practical: what a plan covers, how long it
takes, and what it does not do. Day by Word does not interpret Scripture or
give spiritual, medical, legal, or other professional advice, and a reading
record describes only what you marked.

## Accuracy

Every reference in every plan is checked by software against a published
versification, and plans are reviewed before they are offered. Mistakes
can still happen; please report one through the support page
(daybyword.com/support), and it will be put right.

## Your record

Your reading record belongs to you. It is kept on your device or in your
browser, not on Day by Word's server, so Day by Word cannot recover it if
it is deleted or the device is lost. Settings can export it as a file.
